01 — About

A short version of a longer story.

I started out coordinating dispatch schedules on a whiteboard and ended up designing the system that replaced it. That arc is more or less my whole career: find the part of the business that runs on memory and goodwill, and give it structure.

Today I lead service delivery at Northwind Logistics, where I own the operating cadence for a 60-person org across four regions. My work sits between the people doing the work and the leadership deciding what's next — translating in both directions, and making sure the numbers everyone argues about are actually the same numbers.

Outside of work I mentor two operations analysts through a career switchers' programme, and I'm slowly learning to make a decent sourdough.

Capacity planning · 2024

Rebuilding the capacity model

Overtime was eating the regional margin and nobody could say why. I rebuilt the forecast from shift-level data, exposed the two routes causing 60% of the overrun, and shipped a staffing model the regional leads maintain themselves.
