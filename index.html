01 — About

A short version of a longer story.

I'm an executive assistant and project manager with thirteen years behind me — agency operations, digital marketing, retail banking, and customer support. Eight of those years were customer-facing: US and AU telecommunications, healthcare collections, and banking, including second-level handling of fraud cases and sensitive account matters.

For the past four years I've run day-to-day delivery for a digital marketing agency. That means coordinating four clients and two specialist contractors, chairing weekly status meetings, owning the ticket queue from intake through sign-off, and keeping six WordPress sites and two Salesforce orgs in working order.

The part I'd point at first: I wrote the AI playbooks the team now uses as standard practice, and I build n8n and Zapier workflows that take recurring admin off people entirely — the weekly project status report used to be assembled by hand, and now it isn't. That's the instinct I bring to a role. I'd rather automate the repetitive parts than quietly absorb them.

Discreet with confidential information, organised under competing priorities, and comfortable being the person who tracks whether the thing actually got done.
